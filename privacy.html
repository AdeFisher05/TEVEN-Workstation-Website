TELEN VENTURES - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Welcome to Telen Ventures. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, register an account, book our workspace, or use our internet services. By using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and other applicable Nigerian laws.

Information we collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as data gathered automatically during your use of our workspace and website.

  • Personal Data You Provide:

    Registration Data: Full name, email address, phone number, physical address, and account login credentials.

    Verification Data: Government-issued identification or age verification information to confirm you meet our minimum age requirement of 18 years.

  • Data Collected Automatically:

    Log and Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and pages viewed directly on our website.

    Network Metrics: When connected to our workspace internet, we log bandwidth usage, connection times, and device MAC addresses to manage network health and prevent security threats.

  • Payment Information:

    All financial transactions are handled securely by our integrated, certified third-party payment gateways. Telen Ventures does not collect, view, or store your credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials.

How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Creating, maintaining, and securing your user account.

  • Processing your online bookings, tracking payments, and sending confirmations.

  • Managing workspace capacity, network bandwidth, and ensuring optimal internet speeds.

  • Rescheduling bookings smoothly in the event of government-mandated closures, public unrest, or unforeseen events.

  • Enforcing our Workspace Terms and Conditions and detecting, preventing, or prosecuting illegal online activities (such as fraud, cyberbullying, or cybercrimes).

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Telen Ventures does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We only disclose information under the following specific circumstances:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: We share data with trusted vendors who perform services for us, such as payment processors, web hosting providers, and SMS/email delivery services. These parties are contractually bound to keep your data confidential.

  • Legal Obligations & Law Enforcement: We will disclose your personal data, network logs, or account history to law enforcement agencies (e.g., EFCC, NPF) or regulatory bodies if required to do so by a valid subpoena, court order, or Nigerian law.

  • Safety & Security: We may disclose information if we believe it is necessary to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding violations of our terms, suspected fraud, or potential threats to the safety and property of the Telen Ventures workstation or its users.

Data Security & Storage

We implement strict technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Your information is stored on secure servers with restricted administrative access.

However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are entirely responsible for keeping your account password confidential. Do not share your login details with anyone.

Your Data Protection Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), you have specific rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The Right to Access: You can request copies of the personal data we hold about you.

  • The Right to Correction: You can request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

  • The Right to Erasure: You can request that we delete your personal data under certain conditions, subject to legal or financial record-keeping requirements.

  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to object to or restrict how we process your data.

    • To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection representative using the details provided below.

Children’s Privacy

Telen Ventures strictly prohibits individuals under the age of 18 from utilizing our services or registering an account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If we discover that an account has been created by a minor, the account and all associated data will be deleted immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect updates in our operations or changes to Nigerian data laws. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed.
